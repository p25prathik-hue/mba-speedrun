**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribution: "MBA Speedrun, OML group project, IIM Ahmedabad".

`data/external/units.csv` records only the *order* of chapters or sessions in published textbooks and
syllabi, with paraphrased unit names, as ordering evidence. It reproduces no text from those works. See
`data/external/sources.csv` for full citations and each source's own licence. MIT OpenCourseWare material
is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
